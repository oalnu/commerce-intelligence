# Commerce Intelligence - Management summary

Core purchase cohorts: January 2017-August 2018. Historical Olist data; not current operating performance.

## Findings

- **Promise adherence:** 93.2%; 6,531 late orders from 96,203 eligible deliveries. Median 10.2 days; P90 23.1 days.
- **Peak cohort:** March 2018 had 81.0% on-time delivery and 1,328 late orders from 7,003 eligible deliveries, versus 94.2% in other core months (a 13.1 percentage-point gap). State-standardized rates remain 80.8% versus 94.2%. Eligibility is about 97.1% in both groups. The decline is materially descriptive; its cause is unknown. The month was selected after inspection, and order independence is not guaranteed; the descriptive 95% difference interval (-14.1 to -12.2 pp) is not a causal or preregistered test.
- **Geography:** RJ had 12.1% late delivery versus SP's 4.5%. SP had the larger late count (1,817 versus 1,495). Combined, 50.7% of late orders; volume matters.
- **Review timing:** post-delivery low-rating share was 19.5% for late orders (n=1,907) versus 9.2% for on-time orders (n=89,005). Coverage is only 29.2% of late versus 99.3% of on-time eligible orders. This highly selected sample cannot estimate a delivery effect. The source describes surveys sent upon receipt or when the estimated date is due, so survey timing is tied to the delivery process. Pre-delivery responses must remain separate.
- **Operational stages:** median valid handoff 1.84 days and transit 7.10 days. These stage medians are not additive and use different valid samples.

## Recommended pilots

1. Operations: diagnose RJ/cohort promise gaps before a narrow lane or communication pilot. Measure on-time %, P90, cancellations and promise length against a comparable group.
2. Seller operations: audit the single-seller queue before assigning fault. Seller 7C67E144 warrants inspection (963 eligible; 89 late; 11.0-day valid handoff median), controlling for product/destination mix.
3. Customer experience: report both survey clocks and test proactive communication with a comparison group. Track post-delivery low ratings and review coverage.

No carrier, cost, margin, refund or experimentally verified savings claim is supported. Findings link to `sql/analysis/` and inspectable results in `reports/analysis/`.
